Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Operator note: this document describes how the Asclepio software behaves. Text in [square brackets] must be completed, and the whole document reviewed by qualified legal counsel for your jurisdiction, before launch.

This policy explains cancellations and refunds for services booked or bought through the Asclepio portal. It does not affect your statutory rights as a consumer.

1. Consultations

  • Cancel [24 hours] or more before the appointment: full refund of any amount paid.
  • Cancel less than [24 hours] before, or not attend: [describe charge, or "no charge"].
  • If we cancel or the doctor is unavailable: full refund, or a free rebooking if you prefer.

2. Medicines

  • Unopened medicines in original packaging may be returned within [7 days] with the order ID for a full refund, unless the law prohibits re-dispensing them (for example, refrigerated items or controlled drugs).
  • Wrong, damaged or expired items: full refund or replacement, at your choice.

3. Bed reservations

Reserving a bed in the portal is free, so there is nothing to refund. Ward charges after admission are billed by the hospital under its published tariff.

4. How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the original payment method within [7 working days] of approval. We do not charge any fee to process a refund.
