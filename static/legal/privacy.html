Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Operator note: this document describes how the PulseCare+ software behaves. Text in [square brackets] must be completed, and the whole document reviewed by qualified legal counsel for your jurisdiction, before launch.

This policy explains what personal data the PulseCare+ portal collects, why, how long we keep it, and the rights you have. The data controller (data fiduciary) is the hospital that operates this portal.

1. What we collect and why

We collect only what is needed to run the portal. We do not ask for email addresses, government IDs, payment card details or precise location.

Optional health tips and hospital updates are sent only if you tick the opt-in box. You can change this at any time in My Medical Profile → Privacy & My Data, and every such message includes an unsubscribe link.

2. Who can see your data

  • Hospital staff with a staff account can see patient records to provide care. Staff accounts are created only by the hospital (self-registration requires an invite code).
  • Other patients never see your name or health details. The ward bed map shows other patients only as "Occupied".
  • Service providers: the portal is hosted by [hosting provider, e.g. Vercel Inc.], which processes data on our behalf under a data processing agreement. The portal loads no third-party analytics, advertising, social media, font or icon services.
  • Authorities where the law requires it (for example, notifiable diseases or a court order).

We do not sell your personal data or use it for targeted advertising.

3. Children

Accounts for anyone under 18 must be created by a parent or legal guardian, who records their name and gives consent on the child's behalf. We do not track children's behaviour or show them targeted advertising. If you believe a child's data was provided without valid consent, contact us and we will delete it.

4. How long we keep data

  • Account data: until you ask us to delete your account.
  • Medical records (consultations, prescriptions): for the period required by medical-record law, [e.g. at least 3 years from the last consultation under the Indian Medical Council (Professional Conduct, Etiquette and Ethics) Regulations, 2002, or longer where state law requires — confirm with counsel], then deleted.
  • Billing records: for the period required by tax law, [e.g. 8 years — confirm with counsel].

5. Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the EU/UK GDPR), you can:

  • Access and portability — download everything we hold about you as a JSON file from My Medical Profile → Privacy & My Data → Download my data.
  • Correction — edit your details in My Medical Profile, or ask staff to correct clinical records.
  • Deletion — use Request account deletion. We complete requests within 30 days. Your login and non-clinical personal data are erased; medical records we are legally required to keep are unlinked from your account and deleted when the retention period ends. You will see the outcome in your privacy panel.
  • Withdraw consent — for optional updates at any time; for health-data processing by requesting deletion (we may then be unable to provide online services).
  • Nominate someone to exercise your rights if you die or become incapacitated, by contacting our Grievance Officer.
  • Complain to our Grievance Officer (below) and, if unresolved, to the Data Protection Board of India or your local data protection authority.

6. Security

Passwords are stored as salted hashes. Sign-in uses an HttpOnly session cookie that is sent only over HTTPS in production. Access to patient records is restricted by role, and patients can only open their own consultations and prescriptions. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure; if a breach affects you, we will notify you and the authorities as the law requires.

7. Cookies

We use one strictly necessary session cookie. See the Cookie Policy.

8. Grievance Officer and contact

Privacy questions, complaints and requests are handled by the hospital's Grievance / Data Protection Officer.

Contact the hospital administration at the reception desk, or ask any staff member to pass your request on.

We acknowledge complaints within 48 hours and resolve them within 30 days.

9. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes we will show the new version in the portal and, where required, ask you to accept it again. The version you accepted is stored with your account.
