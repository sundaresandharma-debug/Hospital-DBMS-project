Cookie Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Operator note: this document describes how the PulseCare+ software behaves. Text in [square brackets] must be completed, and the whole document reviewed by qualified legal counsel for your jurisdiction, before launch.

This policy explains the cookies and similar technologies used by the PulseCare+ portal.

1. What we use

NameTypePurposeDuration
sessionStrictly necessary cookie (first party, HttpOnly, SameSite=Lax, Secure in production)Keeps you signed in and protects your accountUntil you close the browser or sign out
pulsecare-cookie-consentBrowser local storage (not a cookie; never sent to our server)Remembers your cookie choice so we don't ask againUntil you clear site data

We do not use analytics, advertising, social media or other tracking cookies, and the portal loads no third-party scripts, fonts or icons — all are served from our own domain.

2. Your choices

Strictly necessary cookies cannot be switched off because the portal cannot work without them. If we ever add optional cookies (such as analytics), they will stay off unless you turn them on, and we will update this policy first. You can review your choice at any time using Cookie settings in the footer of the portal, and you can delete cookies in your browser settings.

3. Contact

Contact the hospital administration at the reception desk, or ask any staff member to pass your request on.
