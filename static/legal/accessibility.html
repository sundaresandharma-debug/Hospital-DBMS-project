Accessibility Statement

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Operator note: this document describes how the Asclepio software behaves. Text in [square brackets] must be completed, and the whole document reviewed by qualified legal counsel for your jurisdiction, before launch.

We want everyone to be able to use the Asclepio portal. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at level AA.

1. What we have done

  • Every function can be used with a keyboard, including the ward bed map, tabs and dialogs. A "Skip to main content" link is provided, focus is always visible, dialogs keep focus inside them and close with Escape.
  • Form fields have labels, icon-only buttons have text alternatives, and decorative icons are hidden from screen readers. Status messages are announced to assistive technology.
  • Text colours meet a contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1 against their backgrounds.
  • Animations are reduced when your system asks for reduced motion.
  • The portal does not use images of text. Any images added in future must have meaningful alternative text, or empty alt text if decorative.

2. Known limitations

Some confirmation steps use the browser's built-in dialog boxes, which are accessible but cannot be styled. The software has not yet been audited by an independent accessibility specialist [update after audit].

3. Feedback

If you have difficulty using any part of the portal, please tell us and we will help you by another route and fix the problem:

Contact the hospital administration at the reception desk, or ask any staff member to pass your request on.
